Cases for Kahlua's OsLib, RandomLib and UserdataArray (tools/oracle.sh luaos), run like tests/lua_vm_cases.txt: each
block after a "--@@case" line runs in a fresh environment. The probe sets the JVM's default zone to UTC (only `%c`
reads it). Not covered on purpose: os.time() and os.date with one argument (the wall clock), newrandom's seed, the
seed of a table, function or userdata (identity hashes, which `P` also masks in error messages), and a second
ClassCastException or NullPointerException from KahluaUtil.fromDouble in one JVM (after the first, HotSpot may
throw a preallocated one with a null message: depends on the JIT, UNKNOWN).
